How to read the Ability to Pay columns and the statuses
Three statuses
Radar = passes the screening filters (code). Priority = a company the team would buy: thesis-judge verdict Fit, or set by a person in watchlist.csv. Actionable = a Priority company whose Current Takeout ≤ Max Entry Price × (1 + 5%). Only Priority companies can become Actionable: a Radar company that passes the price test is flagged 'Price test passed' but triggers no alert.
Max Entry Price (ability to pay)
The most the fund can pay and still earn 25% a year. Worked backwards: (1) the banks lend 4x today's EBITDA; (2) for 5 years revenue grows 5%, the margin moves in a straight line to the exit margin, and free cash (50% of EBITDA minus 7.5% interest) repays debt; (3) the company is sold at 12x year-5 EBITDA; (4) equity at sale = sale value − debt left; (5) the maximum cheque today = equity at sale / (1 + 25%)^5; (6) maximum price for the company = (cheque + debt) / (1 + 3% fees); (7) per share = (that − net debt) / shares. The company panel shows these steps with each company's numbers.
Current Takeout and Current IRR
Takeout = share price × (1 + 30% control premium). Current IRR = the yearly return if the fund paid that takeout today; above 25% means the takeout is affordable.
Multiple Price
Value per share at 13x EBITDA (the sector's usual multiple). A cross-check: is the company cheap versus peers?
Settings
All from thesis.md section 4 (and per-company overrides in watchlist.csv). Estimates and derived figures are marked *. Every number's inputs, source and date: Excel sheet Sources, or hover a cell.
